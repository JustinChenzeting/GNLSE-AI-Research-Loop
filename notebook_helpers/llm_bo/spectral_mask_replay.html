How to read the uncertainty

This GP is a reconstruction for presentation. The original LLMBO selected points directly with the LLM and did not update a GP posterior. These bands are not LLM confidence.

  • Only measurements available at the selected iteration condition the GP. “Before” leaves out the current test; “after” includes it. The pulse panels always show that test’s recorded fields.
  • The horizontal axis follows the recorded masks: every integer is one complete 100-factor point. Curves between them predict interpolated masks snapped to the 0.01 grid. This is a slice through the search, not a physical control or the whole 100-dimensional space.
  • Shading is mean ±1.96σ for the latent GP. The assumed 0.0002 observation standard deviation regularizes conditioning; it is not calibrated measurement noise. Gaussian bands may extend beyond the physical D_NL range [0,1].
